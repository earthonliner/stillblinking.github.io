# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 17, 2026  
**Service / Application Name:** Still Blinking

**Still Blinking** ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in connection with our website and our internal content publishing application (the "Service").

## 1. Information We Collect and Use

Still Blinking is an internal creator tool and static informational website:

- **Public Website Visitors:** We do not require user registration, and we do not collect, store, or process personal data from visitors to this static website. Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages, which may collect standard server logs in accordance with the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Social Media API Integrations (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram):** Our publishing tool connects to official platform APIs (such as the TikTok Content Posting API, YouTube Data API, and Instagram Graph API) solely for the purpose of authenticating our own official creator accounts and uploading/managing our own video content.

## 2. How OAuth Credentials and Tokens Are Handled

- **Scope of Access:** OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are generated exclusively for the account owner of Still Blinking to upload video drafts or posts and retrieve basic account/upload status.
- **Storage & Security:** Credentials and tokens are stored locally on secure, access-restricted machines owned by the creator and are never committed to public repositories or exposed to third parties.

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do **not** sell, rent, trade, or share any personal data or API data with third parties. Data transmitted through platform APIs is sent directly between our publishing tool and the respective platform (e.g., TikTok, YouTube, or Instagram) over encrypted HTTPS connections.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

Authentication tokens are retained only as long as necessary to operate our publishing workflow and can be revoked or deleted at any time by removing local token files or revoking access via the respective platform's security settings (e.g., TikTok Account Permissions, Google Account Security Settings, or Meta Business Integrations).

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
